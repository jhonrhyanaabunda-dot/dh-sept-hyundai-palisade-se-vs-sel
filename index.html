02The entry trim

What the SE gives you.

The SE is the foundation of the Palisade range, and it carries more of the Palisade's value than most shoppers expect.

It includes Hyundai SmartSense, the standard suite of driver-assistance and safety features. It carries the same three-row, eight-passenger body as higher trims. For a buyer who wants the Palisade's space and reputation without paying for extras, the SE is the logical start.

Most SE shoppers are after the size and the warranty more than the toys. That is a reasonable read. The Palisade's value lives in its three-row cabin room and Hyundai's coverage, both of which the SE has in full, including the 10-year/100,000-mile powertrain limited warranty and the 5-year/60,000-mile new-vehicle limited warranty.

03The step-up

What the SEL adds.

The SEL builds on the SE, and it is the trim most families gravitate toward.

Across recent model years, the SEL has typically layered in upgraded interior materials, more capable climate control, additional convenience features, and expanded driver-assist options. It smooths out daily driving without jumping to the price of the Limited or Calligraphy.

If you are weighing the top of the range instead, see our separate Palisade Limited vs Calligraphy comparison. This page stays focused on the SE-to-SEL decision.

A few short facts worth holding onto

  • The SE and SEL share the same core three-row platform.
  • The SEL's upgrades are mostly comfort and convenience, not a different drivetrain in most configurations.
  • Captain's-chair seating, which drops capacity to seven, is frequently an SEL-and-up option.

Confirm the current-year drivetrain and option packages with us or on the Hyundai site, since Hyundai adjusts trim contents annually.

04The decision

How to decide between SE and SEL.

Buy the SE if your priority is the lowest entry price and you do not need the comfort extras. Buy the SEL if daily comfort is worth a modest step up.

08SE vs SEL questions

Frequently asked questions about the Palisade SE and SEL.

What is the main difference between the Hyundai Palisade SE and SEL?

The SE is the entry trim with the core three-row package and Hyundai SmartSense safety. The SEL adds comfort and convenience features such as upgraded interior materials and more advanced climate controls. Both seat up to eight.

Verify the exact current-year feature list on hyundaiusa.com.

Is the Palisade SEL worth the extra cost over the SE?

For daily commuters and full-family households, the SEL's comfort upgrades often justify the step up. Budget-focused buyers who do not need the extras may prefer the SE.

The price gap is usually narrower than buyers expect. Ask us to run both trims against your budget.

Do the Palisade SE and SEL have the same engine?

In most configurations the SE and SEL share the same core drivetrain, with the SEL's upgrades focused on comfort and convenience.

Drivetrain details can change by model year. Confirm the current-year specs on hyundaiusa.com or with our sales team before buying.

How many people can the Palisade SE and SEL seat?

Both trims seat up to eight passengers in the standard three-row layout. Captain's-chair seating, which reduces capacity to seven, is frequently available as an SEL-and-up option.

Confirm the seating configuration on the specific vehicle you are considering.

What warranty comes with a new Hyundai Palisade?

Every new Palisade is backed by Hyundai's warranty program, which our team will explain in writing before you sign. Ask us to detail the current new-vehicle and powertrain coverage terms.
